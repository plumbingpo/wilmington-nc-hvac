Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Wilmington HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Preventive Care

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Wilmington HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
